ReconPro Lite

Browser-based reconciliation — compares your company ledger against a bank or GST statement. Columns are displayed side-by-side per pair for easy manual review. All processing stays on your device.

Zero uploads · Zero storage · Cleared on tab close
How It Works
  • 1Upload Company Ledger (File A) and Bank / GST Statement (File B) — .xlsx / .xls.
  • 2First row of each file must be column headers. Mappings are auto-suggested.
  • 3Review and adjust column pairs. Each pair is shown side-by-side in the results.
  • 4Click Run Reconciliation. Every row from both files gets a status.
  • 💡New: Results are column-interleaved (Ledger col 1 → Bank col 1 → Ledger col 2 → Bank col 2…) for easy row-by-row validation. Dates in any format are normalised before comparison.
